# Simple website

This is the editing guide for the Simple website: a landing page plus sign in, sign up and reset password pages.

## Quick links

- [Pages](cloudcannon:collections/pages)
- [Site settings](cloudcannon:collections/data)

## Pages

**Pages** holds every page on the site. Pages open in the Visual Editor, where you can click text and images on the page to change them. Switch to the Data Editor to see every field in a form.

- **Home page** (`index`) is a landing page built from sections: a hero, a logo cloud, a features grid, a testimonial and a call to action. Add, remove and reorder sections from the **Page sections** list, or with the controls on each section in the Visual Editor.
- **Sign in**, **Sign up** and **Reset password** are account pages. You can change their heading, form labels and placeholders, button text and the text below the form. The forms are for show only; they don't send anything.

To create a page, choose **+ Add** then **Landing page**. A new page shows the home page as its preview until the site rebuilds with it. Its title becomes its web address, so you can rename the file afterwards to change the address.

To delete or rename a page, use the menu on the page in the Pages list. The home page and the three account pages each have their own place on the site, so don't rename or delete them.

## Site settings

These files apply to every page:

- **site** — the default page title and description, for pages that don't set their own.
- **navigation** — the buttons at the top right of every landing page (Login and Register).
- **footer** — the copyright line, the link columns, the social links and their heading, and the large faded word at the bottom of the page.
- **auth** — the animated terminal panel beside the forms on the account pages.

## Images

Upload images through any image field. They're stored with the site's other images.

## Some sections have fixed slots

- The **logo cloud** always shows nine logos, each in its own position. Swap or reorder them, but you can't add or remove one.
- The **terminal** in the hero and on the account pages has six lines that animate in turn. Change their text, but you can't add or remove a line.
- **Feature icons** and **social link icons** come from a fixed set. Pick one from the list.
